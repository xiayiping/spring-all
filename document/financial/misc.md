# A News

Triple Flag Precious Metals Corp. (TFPM) announced an agreement with Evolution Mining Limited to fund $84.3 million in the fourth quarter of 2026 for development of the E44 open pit gold deposit at Northparkes in New South Wales, Australia


what does this news say

This news highlights a financial agreement between **Triple Flag Precious Metals Corp. (TFPM)** and **Evolution Mining Limited**. Here’s a breakdown of the key points:

1. **Parties Involved**:
    - **Triple Flag Precious Metals Corp.**: A company that provides financing to mining projects in exchange for future royalties or metal streams.
    - **Evolution Mining Limited**: A gold mining company involved in developing and operating gold mines.

2. **The Agreement**:
    - TFPM has agreed to provide **$84.3 million** in funding.
    - The funding will be used for the development of the **E44 open pit gold deposit**.

3. **Location**:
    - The E44 open pit gold deposit is part of the **Northparkes mine** in **New South Wales, Australia**.

4. **Timeline**:
    - The funding will occur in the **fourth quarter of 2026**.

5. **Purpose**:
    - The funding is likely structured as part of a **streaming or royalty agreement**, where TFPM will receive a portion of future production (gold or other metals) from the E44 deposit in return for its financial investment.
    - It supports the development of mining infrastructure to extract gold from the E44 open pit.

### Implications:
- **For Triple Flag**: This is an investment in a gold mining project that could provide future revenue through royalties or metal streams.
- **For Evolution Mining**: The funding helps finance the development of the E44 deposit without raising debt or diluting shareholder equity.

In essence, this agreement reflects a collaboration to advance the development of a gold mining project, benefiting both companies strategically and financially.


Let’s clarify the terms **"royalty"** and **"fund"** in the context of this news:

---

### **1. What is a Royalty?**
- A **royalty** is a financial agreement where one party (in this case, Triple Flag Precious Metals Corp.) provides funding for a mining project in exchange for a percentage of the revenue or production from the mine once it becomes operational.
- In this case:
    - **TFPM** is likely funding Evolution Mining's project in return for a **gold royalty** or **streaming agreement**.
    - This means TFPM will receive a portion of the **gold production** or **revenue** from the E44 open pit gold deposit over time, as compensation for its upfront investment.

### **2. What is a Fund?**
- The **fund** here refers to the **$84.3 million** that TFPM is providing to Evolution Mining.
- This money will be used to develop the **E44 open pit gold deposit** at Northparkes, including infrastructure, mining equipment, and other operational costs.
- Essentially, TFPM is financing the development of this mining project.

---

### **How Do Royalties and Funding Work Together?**
- TFPM is not directly operating the mine. Instead, it acts as a financier.
- In return for the funding, TFPM secures the **right to a share of future gold production** or revenue from the E44 deposit. This ensures that TFPM benefits from the mine's success over the long term.

So, **fund** refers to the financial investment TFPM is making, while **royalty** is the future compensation TFPM expects to receive based on the mine’s performance.
