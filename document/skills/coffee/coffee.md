# Types

There are many types of coffee preparation and brewing methods, each offering a unique flavor, texture, and style. Here's a list of popular coffee types categorized by how they are made or served:

---

## **Espresso-based Coffees**
1. **Espresso**: A strong, rich shot of coffee made by forcing hot water through fine coffee grounds.
2. **Cappuccino**: Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam (1:1:1 ratio).
3. **Latte (Caffè Latte)**: A single shot of espresso mixed with a larger amount of steamed milk, topped with a small layer of foam.
4. **Macchiato**: An espresso shot "stained" with a small amount of milk or milk foam.
5. **Americano**: Espresso mixed with hot water to create a milder flavor.
6. **Flat White**: Similar to a cappuccino but with micro-foamed milk and no thick layer of froth.
7. **Mocha**: A combination of espresso, steamed milk, and chocolate (either syrup or cocoa), often topped with whipped cream.

---

## **Classic Brewed Coffees**
1. **Black Coffee**: Plain coffee brewed using methods like drip brewing or a French press, without any added milk or sugar.
2. **Drip Coffee**: Ground coffee brewed by letting hot water drip through a coffee filter into a pot.
3. **French Press (Press Pot)**: Coffee brewed by steeping coarse coffee grounds in hot water and then pressing with a plunger.
4. **Moka Pot**: A stovetop coffee maker that brews coffee by passing boiling water pressurized by steam through ground coffee.
5. **Percolated Coffee**: Coffee brewed continuously as boiling water cycles through coffee grounds in a percolator.

---

## **Cold/Beverage Coffees**
1. **Cold Brew**: Coffee brewed by steeping coarsely ground coffee in cold water for 12-24 hours.
2. **Iced Coffee**: Regular brewed coffee chilled and served over ice.
3. **Nitro Cold Brew**: Cold brew coffee infused with nitrogen gas, giving it a silky texture and a frothy top.
4. **Affogato**: A scoop of ice cream (often vanilla) topped with a shot of hot espresso.

---

## **Special or Regional Coffees**
1. **Turkish Coffee**: Finely ground coffee simmered (not boiled) in a special pot called a cezve, often served unfiltered with cardamom or sugar.
2. **Arabic Coffee**: A spiced coffee made with cardamom, often served in small cups without milk or sugar.
3. **Cuban Coffee (Cafecito)**: Very strong espresso sweetened with sugar and often served in small cups.
4. **Irish Coffee**: Coffee mixed with Irish whiskey, sugar, and topped with lightly whipped cream.
5. **Vietnamese Coffee**: Strong coffee made with condensed milk and a metal drip filter (Phin). It can be served hot or iced.
6. **Cortado**: A Spanish coffee made with equal parts espresso and warm milk, balancing the strength of the coffee with the softness of milk.

---

## **Foam/Texture-based Variations**
1. **Dalgona Coffee**: Whipped coffee made from instant coffee, sugar, and water, layered over cold or hot milk.
2. **Café au Lait**: Equal parts brewed coffee and steamed milk.
3. **Ristretto**: A shorter, concentrated shot of espresso with less water for a richer flavor.
4. **Breve**: An espresso drink made with steamed half-and-half instead of milk.
