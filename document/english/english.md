#### "Stay true to my vision" 

means remaining committed to your goals, ideas, or creative direction, even in the face of challenges, distractions, or opposing influences. It reflects a determination to uphold authenticity and stay aligned with personal values, beliefs, or aspirations, rather than compromising or straying away due to external pressures or trends.

For example:
- An artist might "stay true to their vision" by refusing to alter their style for commercial gain.
- An entrepreneur may stick to their vision of creating ethical products, even if shortcuts could lead to faster profits.

It emphasizes integrity, focus, and perseverance.

#### "Stubborn about your path" 

means being unwavering or resolute in following your chosen direction, goals, or beliefs, even when faced with obstacles, criticism, or differing opinions. It reflects a strong determination to stick to what you believe is right for you, regardless of external pressures or setbacks.

This phrase emphasizes persistence and confidence in your decisions, but it can also suggest being inflexible or resistant to change, even when adapting might be beneficial. The key is finding a balance between being committed to your path and remaining open to growth and learning along the way.

For example:
- A writer might be "stubborn about their path" by refusing to compromise their creative vision, even if it means rejection from publishers.
- An athlete might be resolute in their training schedule, staying focused on their goals despite challenges or distractions.

It's about standing firm in your journey and purpose.


#### "I committed" 

means that you made a firm decision or promise to dedicate yourself to something, such as a goal, task, relationship, or responsibility. It implies taking deliberate action to follow through and invest your time, energy, or effort into what you've chosen.

For example:

- "I committed to finishing this project by the deadline" means you promised to complete the task and are working toward it.
- "I committed to my fitness journey" means you decided to stay dedicated to improving your health.
- "I committed to being honest" means you’ve promised to uphold honesty in your actions.

It reflects a sense of responsibility and determination to honor your decision.
